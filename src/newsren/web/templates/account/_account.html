{%- endmacro %} {#- How Follow This Story / My Newsren monitoring works (plan §2.6, §6). -#} {% macro how_monitoring() -%}

How monitoring works

  • Only material developments that meet your criteria. The agent alerts you when a new evaluation, filing, study, decision or correction matches what you asked for — not every time another article appears. Near-duplicate copies never alert twice.
  • Opinion is excluded unless you opt in. Columns and commentary don't trigger alerts unless your question asks for them or you tick “Include opinion”.
  • Approved sources only. The agent revisits approved sources — regulators, filings, research indexes and vetted newsrooms — never the open web.
  • Automatic delivery is limited to low-risk personal briefings. Your briefing and alerts are the only things sent automatically; published briefs pass editorial review first, and every finance story has a human editor.
  • New follows look forward. A follow alerts on developments from now on, and you can stop it at any time; alerts it already found are kept.
