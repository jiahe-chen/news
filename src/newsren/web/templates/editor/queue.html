{% call m.expandable("How the quality gate works") %}
  1. Automated checks on every brief. Citation coverage and unknown citation ids, approved sources only, well-formed links, retrieval timestamps and licensing notes, disagreements described, preprints and forecasts labelled, studied populations named, finance guardrails (no buy/sell/hold calls, price targets or personal advice) and performance claims labelled backtest, simulated or live.
  2. Routing. Finance stories always come to this desk (plan §4: human editorial review for any finance story). So do briefs with high-impact claims, failed blockers or warnings. {% if require_human_approval %}Human approval is currently required for every brief before publication (plan §6: require human approval for publishing initially).{% else %}Only low-risk, non-finance briefs with no failed blockers publish automatically.{% endif %}
  3. Blockers stop publication. Fix the brief (regenerate it from the story's sources) or approve with notes that start with {{ override_prefix }} followed by your justification; the override and the checks it overrode are logged.
  4. Your judgement is measured. Accurate/Inaccurate verdicts on claims are the sample behind the Trust metric, review time feeds Editorial efficiency, and corrections are published with the brief. See Analytics.
