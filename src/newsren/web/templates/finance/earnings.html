How to read an Earnings Decoder report

Reported, not forecast

Revenue, profit, and earnings per share come from the company's 10-Q or 10-K as filed. The source quote names the XBRL tag, form, filing date, and period for every number.

Like-for-like comparisons

Each quarter is compared with the same quarter a year earlier, which removes seasonal effects, and with the previous quarter. Percentage changes are left out when either figure is a loss or zero.

Guidance is quoted, never inferred
