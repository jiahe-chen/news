How the research agent works

  1. Plans the task. It identifies what your question needs: the original announcement, primary research or filings, and independent evaluations.
  2. Searches approved sources only. The Newsren's archive of vetted publishers, plus primary sources such as research indexes, SEC EDGAR filings, and regulators. No open web browsing.
  3. Reads and cross-checks. It compares what vendors claim with what independent sources found, and notes corrections, preprints, and opinion pieces.
  4. Answers with citations. Every statement links to its source, with publication and retrieval dates.
  5. Reports what it could not verify. Gaps are listed instead of guessed at.

What it won't do

  • Use sources outside The Newsren's approved list.
  • Fill gaps with guesses: unsupported parts of your question are listed as “could not verify”.
  • Give investment advice. Questions about whether to buy, sell, or hold are answered with the news and concepts behind them, never a recommendation.
  • Replace editors: answers are AI-drafted research, not reviewed articles. Published briefs are reviewed before publication.
